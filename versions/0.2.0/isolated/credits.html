GoodVibes Textures

  Copyright (c) Acaitart

  BlockGameTemp version uses assets from the GoodVibes texture pack, some

  of which have been modified and/or renamed:

    - The tab icon is derived from iron_sword.png and iron_pickaxe.png.
    - The oak_leaves.png and oak_leaves_no_alpha.png textures were modified by
      adding color.
    - The coal_ore.png texture was renamed to coal_matrix.png.
    - The acacia_log.png texture was renamed to oak_log.png.
    - The acacia_log_top.png texture was renamed to oak_log_top.png.
    - The redstone_ore.png texture was renamed to carbuncle_matrix.png.
    - The grass_block_side.png texture was renamed to sod_block_side.png.
    - The grass_block_top.png texture was renamed to sod_block_top.png.
    - The acacia_planks.png texture was rotated and renamed to
      oak_boards_rotated.png.
    - The pickaxe model is derived from iron_pickaxe.png.
    - The magma.png texture was derived from lava_still.png.
    - The main_menu_background.png image is derived from coal_ore.png,
      coal_block.png, iron_ore.png, peridot_ore.png, redstone_ore.png, and
      stone.png.
    - The carbuncle_in_matrix.glb model was derived from the redstone_ore.png
      texture.
    - The coal_in_matrix.glb model was derived from the coal_ore.png texture.
    - The dirt_block.glb model was derived from the dirt.png texture.
    - The hematite_block.glb model was derived from the iron_ore.png texture.
    - The oak_boards.glb model was derived from the acacia_planks.png texture.
    - The oak_leaf_block.glb model was derived from the oak_leaves.png texture.
    - The oak_log.glb model was derived from the acacia_log.png and 
      acacia_log_top.png textures.
    - The sand_block.glb model was derived from the sand.png texture.
    - The snow_block.glb model was derived from the snow.png texture.
    - The stone_block.glb model was derived from the stone.png texture.
