GoodVibes Textures

  Copyright (c) Acaitart

  BlockGameTemp version uses assets from the GoodVibes texture pack, some

  of which have been modified and/or renamed:

    - The tab icon is derived from iron_sword.png and iron_pickaxe.png.

    - The oak_leaves.png texture was modified by adding color.

    - The coal_ore.png texture was renamed to coal_matrix.png.

    - The acacia_log.png texture was renamed to oak_log.png.

    - The acacia_log_top.png texture was renamed to oak_log_top.png.

    - The redstone_ore.png texture was renamed to carbuncle_matrix.png.

    - The grass_block_side.png texture was renamed to sod_block_side.png.

    - The grass_block_top.png texture was renamed to sod_block_top.png.

    - The acacia_planks.png texture was rotated and renamed to

      oak_boards_rotated.png.

    - The pickaxe model is derived from iron_pickaxe.png.

    - The magma.png texture was derived from lava_still.png.

    - The main_menu_background.png image is derived from coal.png,

      coal_block.png, iron_ore.png, peridot_ore.png, redstone_ore.png, and

      stone.png.
